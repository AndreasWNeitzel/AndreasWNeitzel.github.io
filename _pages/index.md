My thesis, Machine Learning Applied to Galactic Archaeology, is co-advised by Tiago L. Campante, Diego Bossini and Andrea Miglio. My 2025 paper in *Astronomy and Astrophysics* introduced a manifold-learning method (UMAP and HDBSCAN) that separates thin-disc, thick-disc, halo and accreted populations, validated on synthetic *Gaia* DR3-like samples.

I develop the machine-learning pipelines of the FCT exploratory project ArqueoGal (2024.15303.PEX, PI T. L. Campante), and spent six months of 2025 to 2026 at INAF-OAS Bologna building simulation pipelines for the proposed *HAYDN* mission.

I am a member of the ESA *Ariel* Science Consortium (Stellar Characterisation working group) and the *Kepler* Asteroseismic Science Consortium, and an affiliate of the *TESS* Asteroseismic Science Operations Centre.
