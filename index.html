Research

The lines of work
A&A 695, A243 (2025), first author · Starfold 0.2.0, open source

Stellar populations without labels, from the paper to Starfold

Manifold learning separates disc, halo and accreted stars in a simulated Milky Way; Starfold is the package that grew out of it, with 17 named warnings.

Open
PhD work within the FCT project ArqueoGal, 2025 to 2027

JANUS, the chemistry of red giants from Gaia spectra

Normalising-flow networks that read stellar parameters and chemistry from Gaia spectra, with a reliability score for every star.

Open
FCT exploratory project, 2026 to 2027, co-investigator

ArqueoGal, the Milky Way's history with Galactic archaeology

The project JANUS and Starfold serve. I develop its machine-learning pipelines and the repository that prepares Gaia DR3 and APOGEE DR19 data with provenance.

Open
INAF Bologna secondment, 2025 to 2026

HAYDNsim, noise forecasts for crowded clusters

Builds synthetic fields of dense star clusters and forecasts each star's photometric noise for the proposed HAYDN mission.

Open
