Stellar Explorer

One star of one solar mass at solar metallicity, from pre-main-sequence contraction to white-dwarf cooling, computed with MESA r24.08.1, with oscillation modes from GYRE. The run stored 94 interiors, and every panel draws the selected model's own. The track line is the run's full history of 13,024 models.

Panels

timeline
the 94 models in order against fractional radius: convective zones in blue, nuclear burning in orange. Drag to move along the track.
HR diagram
effective temperature against luminosity, with lines of constant radius. Click a model.
interior
a cut through the star coloured by temperature, with convective cells, radiative hairlines and the burning regions; a ruler marks the zone boundaries, and a detail circle magnifies the centre when the structure there is too small to see. The shells move with the selected oscillation mode, at an exaggerated amplitude and one period per four seconds.
profiles
six quantities from the model's profile against fractional radius; convective zones shaded blue, burning regions orange.
oscillations
the propagation diagram (Brunt-Väisälä and Lamb frequencies, with the selected mode's cavities shaded), the échelle diagram, the normalised mode inertia, and the mode's radial displacement weighted by r√ρ (the quantity whose square integrates to the mode energy), a JWKB reconstruction on the GYRE equilibrium model rather than GYRE's own eigenfunction. Click a mode in the échelle or inertia panel to select it.

Sources

MESA: Paxton et al. 2011, ApJS 192, 3. GYRE: Townsend and Teitler 2013, MNRAS 435, 3406. The data file (HDF5, 12 MB) is read in the browser from github.com/AndreasWNeitzel/stellar-explorer-data. Eight models, at the helium flash and along the asymptotic giant branch, have no GYRE output in the run.
