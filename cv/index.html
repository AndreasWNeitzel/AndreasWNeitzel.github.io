  4. May 2026 proposal

    PLATO noise estimates for a blue-straggler guest-observer proposal (34 open clusters)

    Co-investigator

    Computed single-camera PLATO noise-to-signal ratios with the HAYDN Noise Estimator for blue stragglers in their host clusters, using Gaia DR3 fields and the PLATO camera configuration.

    • 124 blue stragglers in 34 host clusters, plus two anchor globular clusters; the target list came from the proposing collaborator.
    • The same Deucalion sweep covered all 36 clusters (34 hosts plus the anchors NGC 1851 and NGC 2298): one HNE run per cluster, 108 Slurm submissions including resubmissions.
    • Delivered as a data package with per-cluster dashboards and combined Gaia, StarHorse and noise tables.
    • Ran noise-to-signal simulations with the HAYDNsim pipeline, with the HAYDN Noise Estimator in its PLATO configuration.
  5. May 2026 proposal

    PLATO noise estimates for the NGC 1851 red-giant guest-observer proposal

    Co-investigator

    Computed PLATO noise-to-signal ratios for the red giants of the globular cluster NGC 1851 with the HAYDNsim pipeline and the HAYDN Noise Estimator in its PLATO configuration.

    • May 2026 proposal

      PLATO noise estimates for accreted-star candidates for a guest-observer proposal

      Co-investigator

      Computed per-candidate PLATO noise-to-signal ratios and flux contamination for accreted-star candidates, each with its own Gaia DR3 field of contaminants.

      • 4,086 candidates, each with a 63 arcsec Gaia DR3 cone to G = 18.5, StarHorse and GSP-Phot parameters and a dedicated noise run.
      • Ran noise-to-signal simulations with the HAYDNsim pipeline, with the HAYDN Noise Estimator in its PLATO configuration.

    Positions

    1. Sep 2025 to Mar 2026 abroad

      Visiting PhD Student, INAF Osservatorio di Astrofisica e Scienza dello Spazio

      INAF-OAS Bologna · Bologna, Italy

      Six-month secondment in A. Miglio's group building the HAYDNsim simulation pipeline for the proposed HAYDN mission, with regular visits to INAF Osservatorio Astronomico di Padova.

      • Built the noise-estimation and synthetic-catalogue tools (TRILEGAL, King-profile sampling) for globular and open clusters.
      • Ran the noise simulations on the Deucalion supercomputer and the University of Bologna clusters.
    2. Sep 2022 to present position

      PhD Researcher, Stellar Astrophysics Group

      IA/CAUP · Porto, Portugal

      Doctoral research on machine-learning methods for Galactic archaeology with Gaia, APOGEE and asteroseismic data.

      • Developing JANUS, a neural network that predicts APOGEE-like stellar parameters and abundances from Gaia XP spectra, with a reliability score for each star.
      • Released Starfold, an open-source package that finds stellar populations without labels and measures how often each one recurs.
      • Developer of the machine-learning pipelines of the FCT exploratory project ArqueoGal (2024.15303.PEX), PI T. L. Campante.
      • Built a characterisation pipeline that combines Gaia DR3, TESS, Kepler/K2 and 2MASS data with PARAM to estimate stellar ages.
    3. Sep 2021 to Mar 2022 position

      MSc Research Fellow, BreakStarS project

      IA/CAUP · Porto, Portugal

      Implemented the small-frequency-separation routine in the stellar characterisation code PARAM.
