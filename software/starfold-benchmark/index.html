Starfold held-out benchmark

Starfold 0.2.0 was tested on ten tables with known classes and lost to the library defaults on two of the four held-out tables.

Starfold chooses UMAP and HDBSCAN settings without labels and reports how far its groups can be trusted. The benchmark checks whether the chosen setting recovers known classes better than the library defaults. Six development tables informed the selection rules, and four held-out tables were not used until the rules were fixed.

Recovery is the adjusted Rand index (floored at zero) against the true classes on the assigned rows, times the share of rows assigned. The defaults score is the median over eight seeds, and a difference below 0.02 counts as a match. Starfold matched or beat the defaults on 6 of 6 development tables and on 2 of 4 held-out tables, losing on COIL-20 and Isolet.
