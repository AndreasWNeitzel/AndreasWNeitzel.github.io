I am a fourth-year PhD candidate at IA and the University of Porto, supervised by Tiago L. Campante, Diego Bossini and Andrea Miglio. My work chains neural networks on Gaia spectra, unsupervised population methods and asteroseismology from TESS and Kepler. I hold the FCT allocations on Deucalion and MareNostrum 5 as responsible investigator.

Before the PhD I did the MSc in Astronomy and Astrophysics in Porto, with an Ariel dissertation, and the BSc in Physics. I spent six months of 2025 and 2026 at INAF-OAS Bologna writing HAYDNsim, the noise-simulation pipeline for the proposed HAYDN mission, and observed with ESPRESSO at the VLT in May 2024.

nowBuilding JANUS, a neural network for the chemistry of oscillating red giants from Gaia spectra, for the FCT project ArqueoGal. Thesis due in 2027.

What I work with

Software engineering

Typed, tested Python packages with continuous integration, locked environments and Sphinx manuals.

Visualisation and web

matplotlib and Plotly figures, and interactive browser applications in WebGL, Canvas2D and SVG.

Machine learning

Normalising-flow ensembles in PyTorch, gradient boosting, manifold learning (UMAP) and density-based clustering (HDBSCAN), with Optuna search.

Uncertainty and reliability

Isotonic calibration of per-star error probabilities, interval-coverage and selection tests, nearest-neighbour novelty for out-of-distribution stars, and covariance propagation through wavelet projections.

Data at scale

Ingestion of whole Gaia releases into Parquet shards, with manifest-resolved datasets, duplicate checks and provenance on every product.

High-performance computing

Slurm on the ARM, x86 and GPU partitions of Deucalion and the University of Bologna clusters, with CUDA and RAPIDS.

Astronomy data and models

Gaia, APOGEE, GALAH, LAMOST, TESS and Kepler data with PARAM, MESA, GYRE, TRILEGAL, galpy and astropy.
