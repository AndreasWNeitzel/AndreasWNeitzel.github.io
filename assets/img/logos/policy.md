# Logo use on this site

Checked on 2026-10-25 against each owner's published terms, for a personal, non-commercial
academic website that names the institutions, facilities and missions behind the work. Quotes
are copied from the pages or PDFs at the URLs given, with typographic quotation marks
normalised (Portuguese and Italian originals carry a one-line English gloss). Every linked URL
answered when checked. Only the logos marked "OK to use" are in this folder; nothing was
downloaded for the others. A logo that an owner offers for download without terms is treated as
"use only with permission": a published file is not a licence, and a trademark is not covered by
an image licence.

### Conditions for the two cleared logos

- **FCT.** Use the files unaltered: positive on light backgrounds, negative on dark ones, a clear
  space of one letter "F" width all round, and at least 100 px wide for signature A on screen
  (manual, p. 11). Signature A is the main version (manual, p. 4). Place it where FCT-funded work
  is presented, next to the written funding mention (see "Funding and computing
  acknowledgements" below). The RNCA acknowledgement template now names the funder
  "FCT I.P./AI²", while the FCT brand page (updated 17 June 2026) still offers this 2022 logo;
  recheck the brand page before publishing.
- **EuroHPC JU.** Use unaltered, with its clear space, and not combined with another logo
  (logo user manual). Show it only in an acknowledgement that the work ran on Deucalion and
  MareNostrum 5, which EuroHPC JU co-funds, never in a row of affiliations: the computing time
  came through FCT's national advanced-computing calls (CPCA), not a EuroHPC access call.

contains the ESA logo, and the ESA notice above requires prior written authorisation for any

## Funding and computing acknowledgements

No FCT rule found requires a logo on a personal website as such, but the written mentions
below apply wherever the site presents work done with the support.

FCT research fellowship regulation (RBI, consolidated 2025), Article 30(1),
<https://www.fct.pt/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/RBi_Republicado_2025.pdf>:
> "Deve ser expressa a menção de apoio financeiro da FCT e o respetivo programa de financiamento em todas as atividades de I&D direta ou indiretamente financiadas pela FCT, assim como em todas as comunicações, publicações e criações científicas, bem como teses, realizadas com os apoios previstos neste Regulamento."

(FCT's financial support and the funding programme must be stated in all communications,
publications and theses produced with the fellowship.) Article 30(2) adds the programme and EU
emblems when the support is EU co-funded (FSE or FEDER); whether the doctoral fellowship
2025.01843.BD was co-funded by FSE+ is not confirmed.

FCT publicity norms for national-funds beneficiaries,
<https://www.fct.pt/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Normas_de_Informacao_e_Publicitacao_de_Apoios_para_Beneficiarios_OE.pdf>:
> "No contexto dos projetos de investigação financiados pela FCT total ou parcialmente por Fundos Nacionais, é necessária a utilização do logótipo da FCT como entidade financiadora, em todos os documentos, publicações ou comunicações resultantes dos apoios, assim como a referência ao financiamento, nos termos acima descritos."

(In FCT-funded research projects, the FCT logo as funder and the funding reference are required on
all documents, publications and communications resulting from the support.) Its website rule
targets the funded action's own website, so a personal site is not clearly covered; showing the
FCT logo with the funding sentence on pages about FCT-funded work (the fellowship, ArqueoGal)
satisfies it at no cost.

FCT advanced-computing calls (CPCA), call notice FCT/CPCA/2025/01, section 10.2:
> "Todas as publicações e teses resultantes da utilização dos recursos computacionais abrangidos pelo presente regulamento bem como quaisquer outros resultados, deverão incluir na seção dos apoios referência à FCT I.P., à plataforma utilizada e ao identificador atribuído ao projeto."

(Publications, theses and any other results from the allocations must name FCT, the platform and
the project identifier.) No logo is required. A plain listing of the allocation is not a result;
a page presenting results computed on Deucalion or MareNostrum 5 is. RNCA's suggested wording
(<https://arquivo.pt/wayback/20261022193948/https://rnca.fccn.pt/publicacoes/>, punctuation
adapted to the site's rules):

- Deucalion: "This work was supported by FCT I.P./AI² under Project <Reference>, DOI <URL> (if known), at Deucalion supercomputer, jointly funded by EuroHPC JU and Portugal."
- MareNostrum 5: "This work was supported by FCT I.P./AI² under Project <Reference>, DOI <URL> (if known), at MareNostrum 5 supercomputer, jointly funded by EuroHPC JU, Portugal, Turkey and Spain."

## Honest alternatives for the marks not cleared

- **A text wordmark row in the site's own type.** Set the names in Geist Mono, uppercase, about
  0.78 rem with 0.06 em tracking in the muted colour (the style of the footer headings), each
  linked to the institution: IA, U.PORTO, FCUP, INAF-OAS. This states the affiliation without
  borrowing anyone's mark, and it looks the same in light and dark mode.
- **Facilities and data as words, not badges.** "Observed with ESPRESSO at ESO's Very Large
  Telescope"; "*Gaia* DR3 (ESA)"; "*Kepler* and *TESS* (NASA)"; "*Ariel* and *PLATO* (ESA)". Mission
  names stay in italics, per the site's style rules.
- **Photographs the site already holds.** His own Paranal photographs say "ESO" more credibly than
  the logo would, and they are his to use.
- **Ask where it is easy.** IA and U.Porto are his own institutions and are likely to agree; INAF
  allows outreach use on written request to the INAF-OAS director; ESO's Department of
  Communication (information@eso.org) and ESA corporate branding (corporatebranding@esa.int)
  handle logo requests. Ask only for a mark he would actually show, and keep the reply on file.
